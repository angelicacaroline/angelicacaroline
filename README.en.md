I am a Machine Learning Engineer focused on **Back-end**, **Machine Learning**, and **infrastructure**, with a special passion for **Embedded Systems** and **Electronics**.

In my day-to-day work, I take care of code like a rancher takes care of a ranch: I round up bugs, tame data across different business territories, and build infrastructure as solid as ironwood. In my repository, the system is rugged and the code is clean.

I am passionate about *agribusiness* and *technology*, and I like bringing references from the field into the way I think and solve problems: observe the terrain, understand the conditions, work with the available resources, and build solutions that actually work in practice.

Here you'll find a bit of this mix of **Machine Learning**, **Back-end**, **Cloud**, **Data**, **DevOps**, **Electronics**, and **Embedded Systems** — always driven by the curiosity to learn something new and turn real-world problems into technological solutions.
